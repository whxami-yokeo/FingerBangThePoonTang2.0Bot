# Privacy Policy for Finger Bang The Poon Tang 2.0

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Finger Bang The Poon Tang 2.0 (“the Bot,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Discord bot operated by
Eddie Menard. This Privacy Policy explains what information the
Bot processes, why it processes it, how long it keeps it, and how you can
request deletion.

By inviting or using the Bot, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Process

Depending on the Bot's enabled features and your use of its commands, we may
process the following Discord-provided information:

- Discord user IDs, usernames, display names, and avatar URLs.
- Discord server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and server configuration settings.
- Command, interaction, and event data necessary to respond to a command or
  provide a Bot feature.
- Message content only when required for a specific Bot feature and only when
  the Bot has been granted access to that content.
- Moderation-related data, such as warnings, actions, reasons, timestamps, and
  the IDs of involved users and moderators, if moderation features are enabled.
- Technical logs, such as error messages, timestamps, command names, and
  limited diagnostic information used to maintain and secure the Bot.

The Bot does not collect Discord passwords, payment-card information, or other
login credentials.

## How We Use Information

We process information only as necessary to:

- Provide, operate, and respond to the Bot's commands and features.
- Save server-specific settings chosen by authorized server administrators.
- Perform moderation, logging, leveling, ticket, reminder, or other features
  that a server has enabled.
- Diagnose errors, prevent abuse, protect the Bot, and improve reliability.
- Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information or use Discord data for advertising,
marketing profiles, or data-broker services.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

We may share information only when necessary with:

- Service providers that host the Bot, database, backups, logging, or error
  monitoring, solely to operate and secure the Bot.
- Discord, when required to provide Bot functionality through Discord's API.
- Law enforcement, regulators, or other parties when disclosure is required by
  applicable law or necessary to protect legal rights, users, or the Bot.

## Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as it is necessary for the Bot's stated
functionality.

- Server configuration data is retained while the Bot remains in the server,
  unless an administrator deletes it sooner.
- Temporary command and interaction data is normally discarded after the
  command has been processed.
- Technical and error logs are retained indefinitely.
- Moderation records, warnings, tickets, reminders, levels, or other
  feature-specific records are retained until deleted by an authorized server
  administrator, the affected user where applicable, or indefinitely.
- When the Bot is removed from a server, associated server data is deleted
  by owner discretion, unless retention is required by law or needed to
  resolve a security, abuse, or legal issue.
